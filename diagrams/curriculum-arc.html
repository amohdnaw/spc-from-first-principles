The twelve-level arc

Five levels exist. Seven do not. This page is the map of how a reader with no statistics gets to designing an experiment, what each level owes the one after it, and which hops have traps in them.

The chain — three parts, twelve levels

Part I · Foundations · video
LEVEL 1Variation new act Nothing repeats. Two identical parts differ, and many parts make a shape nobody chose.
can scatter be predicted?
LEVEL 2Chance new act Probability as long-run frequency. What a claim like “0.27 %” is a claim about.
how do you summarise a shape in two numbers?
LEVEL 3Centre and spread exists · level-0 The mean is a balance point; spread has to be squared first; why the divisor is n−1.
which of those two do you monitor?
LEVEL 4The average is predictable exists · level-1 One part is noise, many parts are information. CLT, and σ⁄√n.
how sure is any one estimate?
LEVEL 5Estimation and uncertainty new act Standard error becomes a confidence interval — including for the constants the rest of the arc uses.
when is a value far enough away to act on?
Part II · The control chart · video
LEVEL 6Limits are a hypothesis test exists · level-2 ±3σ is not taste, it is a bet: 99.73 % inside, one false alarm per 370 subgroups.
what else counts as evidence?
LEVEL 7Evidence and decisions new act α and β, p-values, power — and the Western Electric rules as evidence rules. More rules buy sensitivity with false alarms, and the trade is arithmetic.
in control, but good enough — compared to what?
LEVEL 8Capability exists · level-3 Cp, Cpk, ppm. Voice of the process against voice of the customer.
how fast would you notice if it moved?
LEVEL 9Detection exists · level-4 ARL. Shewhart forgets every point, EWMA remembers, and memory wins on drift.
all of this assumed measured, continuous, normal data. What if it isn’t?
Part III · Applied · interactive labs
LEVEL 10Counting, not measuring new lab Binomial and Poisson. p, np, c and u charts, chart selection, rational subgrouping. Annex: capability when the distribution is not normal.
what drives the variation you are watching?
LEVEL 11Relationships new lab Least squares, residuals, R², prediction intervals — then ANOVA as variance decomposition, which is the same arithmetic a Gage R&R runs on.
can you cause the effect on purpose?
LEVEL 12Experiments new lab Factorial DOE. Interactions are why one-factor-at-a-time fails. Screening, then response surface.
SIBLING SITEMSA own contract first Measurement as its own process: repeatability against reproducibility, GR&R by ANOVA, %GRR and ndc, bias, linearity, stability, attribute agreement.

The traps in the hops

Renumbering reaches into the videos

Fifteen places name a level number, and they are not all HTML. Title cards read “Level 2 · ±3σ is not taste”, and narration speaks “Level one told us which distribution every subgroup mean is drawn from”. Renaming files alone leaves nine acts contradicting their own pages.

This is the last cheap moment

Renumbering costs a rebuild and nothing else, because the voice is local and free. Do it after any line is recorded in Ammar’s own voice and those takes are wasted. Renumber first, record later.

The old URLs are already published

level-0…4.html are live on Pages and cited in an open PR against portfolio-site. Every old path keeps a stub that redirects to its new number and carries rel="canonical", or shared links start 404-ing.

Empty slots must hold still

A twelve-slot map with five things in it only works if the numbers are frozen before anything is authored against them. Moving one topic later renumbers everything downstream a second time.

The MSA boundary is a rule, not a preference

The SPC site never teaches GR&R; the MSA site never teaches control limits. They link once each, at the seam: Level 11 hands over mid-thought on variance decomposition, and MSA hands back to Level 8 with “a Cpk is only as good as the gage”.

Part III is labs by decision

Not a shortfall. DOE at the acts’ quality bar is weeks of authoring, and a surface you drag teaches an interaction better than a video of one. Video stays available for anything that later earns it.
