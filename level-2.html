Level II

Control limits are a hypothesis test

±3σ isn't taste. It's a bet at 370:1 odds.

If a process is stable, Level I's logic tells us exactly which distribution every subgroup mean is drawn from. Choose limits at ±3σ of that distribution and you've defined a test: points inside are boring, a point outside happens 0.27% of the time by pure chance — once per ~370 subgroups. So when one appears, it's rational to bet something changed.
